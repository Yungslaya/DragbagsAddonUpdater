# Know your next profession step

Open **/prof** to see what to craft or gather next, which materials you have, and where to find your next trainer. ProfessionPal brings your guide into WoW Forever so you can spend more time playing and less time switching between windows.

## Pick a material. Find a place to farm.

Farm Planner covers **Mining, Herbalism and Skinning**. Browse by material or zone, compare suggested areas, and open the map for your chosen destination.

- Ore and herb maps show selected recorded spawn locations and a gathering loop.
- Skinning recommendations identify hunting areas, with mapped destinations where supported and written directions elsewhere.
- New Skinning options include **Riverglades Heavy/Thick Leather** and **Zephras Isle Light Leather**.
- Clear directions help you choose an area appropriate to your skill. Classic guide rankings are identified as such.

Mapped spots are possible spawn locations, not a live view of available nodes or beasts. Farm Planner recommendations do not change your profession leveling guide.

## Works with Market Ledger

See scanned Auction House price estimates for ore, herbs and leather directly in Farm Planner. Hover an estimate to check its scan age; estimates at least a day old are marked stale.

Install [Market Ledger](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/market-ledger), open it at the Auction House, and use **Scan Bags**. Select a material in ProfessionPal's **By material** view to see an available estimate. Materials without usable scan data show **No price data**.

Requires Market Ledger 1.3.7 or newer and ProfessionPal 1.3.8.4 or newer. Prices are observed asking prices, not guaranteed sales. Market Ledger is optional; ProfessionPal works without it.

## More help while you play

- Guides for all 12 professions, with material counts, training reminders and trainer directions.
- Shopping lists and crafted gear suggestions.
- A compact tracker and gathering HUD to keep useful information nearby.
- Guide import and optional recipe learning at trainers.

## Optional integrations

- **Shortest Path Forever:** travel routing for supported map destinations.
- **TomTom:** trainer arrows.
- **Market Ledger:** Auction House decisions and scanned material estimates.

## Start here

1. Install ProfessionPal and restart WoW.
2. Type **/prof** and choose your profession.
3. Open **Farm Planner** when you want to choose a material or farming area.

For a problem report, include your addon version, profession skill, and what you clicked. **/prof debug** opens optional diagnostics; **/prof debug off** turns capture off.
