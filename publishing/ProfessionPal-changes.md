# ProfessionPal 1.3.8.4

- Added Skinning to Farm Planner, with leather farming recommendations and clearer skill guidance.
- Added Riverglades Heavy/Thick Leather and Zephras Isle Light Leather farming options.
- Corrected the Darkshore Skinning destination. Areas without a mapped destination now give written directions instead of a zone-center pin.
- Improved map directions and Shortest Path Forever route switching. Skinning directions no longer show controls for a gathering loop that does not exist.
- Farm Planner shows Auction House price estimates when Market Ledger 1.3.7 or newer is installed. Hover for scan age; estimates at least a day old are marked stale.
- Added an optional, dismissible explanation of Market Ledger prices.
- Added `/prof debug` for reporting ProfessionPal problems.

Profession leveling and material requirements are unchanged. Auction House estimates are asking prices, not guaranteed sales.

Fully restart WoW after updating from 1.3.8 because this update adds a new addon file. Scan Bags in Market Ledger to populate prices and scan times.
